# History Atlas — Product One-Pager

## Brand

**History Atlas**

Domain: `historyatlas.net`  
Internal codename: **Project Alexandria**  
Tagline: **History has coordinates.**

## Vision

Create a platform where human history can be explored through time, space, and relationships.

## Problem

Historical knowledge is fragmented:

- maps often lack time,
- encyclopedias are text-first,
- public historical GIS data is dispersed,
- AI explanations are often disconnected from structured, source-traceable spatial and temporal data.

## Solution

History Atlas will progressively combine:

- historical globe and maps,
- timeline,
- structured historical datasets,
- provenance and download,
- search,
- creator tools,
- source-grounded AI explanations,
- APIs.

## First product

A public History Globe displaying historical boundaries with simple interaction.

## Platform audience

Everyone interested in history.

## Initial go-to-market user

History YouTubers and other creators who repeatedly need understandable historical maps and visual assets.

## First user promise

Find and understand a historical map faster than searching, cleaning, and styling raw GIS files manually.

## Long-term core asset

**History Data Hub**: normalized datasets plus provenance, licensing, time coverage, spatial coverage, entities, geometries, and relationships.

## Business model hypotheses

Not required for MVP:

- creator export subscription,
- premium visual presets,
- education licenses,
- dataset/API access,
- sponsorship and contextual advertising,
- source-grounded AI tools.

## Product principles

1. Think in decades. Build in days.
2. Launch before building a perfect platform.
3. Preserve provenance.
4. Separate platform identity from initial customer acquisition.
5. Accumulate reusable data assets.
6. Add complexity only when users justify it.
